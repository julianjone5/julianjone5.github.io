My name is Julian Jones, I was born in Cape Town South Africa in 1995. I have 2 loving parents who raised me and decided to move from South Africa to Vancouver Canada when I was 5 years old. This was a pivotal moment in my life because I left behind the comfort and familiarity of my family in South Africa, to start a new life in Canada.
### From Humble Beginnings

I grew up as an only child and loved math and science. I spent a lot of time alone, and as a child loved playing video games. I had a Gameboy color, and a Gamecube as a kid and I loved playing adventure rpg games that required strategy like Pokemon, and the Legend of Zelda. I also liked socializing with friends, who since I didn't have family nearby became a second home for me.
### Highschool

In highschool I had a lot of friends and loved playing basketball, and cross country. I was competitive and went to Provincials for cross country running. To this day I still love running and find it very peaceful, and good for the soul.

I found school fairly straightforward, but struggled with one particular subject in Highschool, Physics 11. Looking back I realize this was all because the preparation I had in grade 10 science was inadequate. This was unfortunate because I excelled in math, and was in math honors 8, and 9. Even worse I loved science, and until that one experience I thought I was going to be a scientist.

After I finished highschool, I took 1 year to work before studying. This was one of the most influential years of my life as I started a cold sales job. This experience pushed me far outside my comfort zone as I was talking to strangers daily, and building rapport quickly. I did enjoy some of it but I was too curious and found the job itself too repetitive to stay.

That's when I decided to go to University.

### College and University

I attended Douglas College for continuing studies, where I took a myriad of courses, but my core initial interest was Philosophy. After doing that for a while I think I wanted more than just hypothesizing and rational arguments. That's when I went all in on Engineering / Physics.

I started with Calculus 1, and boy was that a rude awakening. Given I hadn't really touched any rigorous math since pre-calc 12, which was this point was 2 years ago I was in way over my head. I tried really hard but with so many core pieces missing from my foundation I failed the final exam and failed the whole class. This is where found the inner courage though to not let a devastating failure stop me from moving forward and pursuing the goal.

In fact not only did I not let that stop me, I doubled down and persuaded my way into Calculus 2 the proceeding semester. I knew this was an absolute insane play, but I knew the level of work that was expected now, so I went all in. I came out of that class with a B+ and was one of the highest grades.

After that I felt invincible, not only that but the inner child in me began learning about all of the interesting science which described our world. I truly loved university and all of the interesting things I learned in my time as an academic.
### Career

My first year out of university was 2019, the year that covid hit. I tried my best to find a job at that time but the job market during that particular period was particularly rough.

To quote Elon Musk here " I started a business because I couldn't get a job". In 2019 cloud technology was firmly implemented and there were a lot of opportunities for skilled software engineers to build things of value. I taught myself how the frontend, and backend worked and how to connect scripts together to achieve things I wanted.

I built a very hacky MVP, and launched it. Looking back I made the same failure that most startup founders make to ship a product without distribution. Not only that but my coding skills were very green so when things started breaking it got overwhelming quickly.

By some luck though while I was sending resumes I was contacted by a then small software startup called Noibu. They had 26 employees and were interested in making me the 27th, I was interested, the only catch was that it was in Ottawa Ontario.

This was a painful decision as I had to leave all of my friends and family behind to pursue my next chapter. This was a tough decision but in reality nothing comes without some sacrifice. So I embarked on my next adventure in Ottawa.

### Noibu and Google

This was the perfect first real tech job for me to transition into. I was used to grinding 15 hour days during covid, and although the startup moved quickly the pace was slower than I was used to. I was in a close team, surrounded by great people that cared about me and that I would help daily build something great.

I spent just over a year there when something unexpected happened. I got a message request via cold email. It was a recruiter, and they were reaching out on behalf of Google. At first I was a little bit stunned, and I didn't really believe it.

This lead to 7 long interviews and many months to complete. But, upon completing the interviews they offered me a role as a TSE, to which 4 years later I am still employed by.

### Present

I love this role and am learning new things about technology every day. However, I still have that internal fire that inside me knows there is more I have left to accomplish. I have things I have to build, and value I need to deliver to people.

I am excited for the recent things I am learning lately about entrepreneurship and marketing. I am excited to eventually leave Google and go all in on my own business again. Although when I do leave Google and go all in on a business I am never planning to work for anyone else ever again.
